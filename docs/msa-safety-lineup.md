## Display Order

### 1. MSA Fire and Rescue Helmets

Recommended sorting logic:

1. Fire helmets
2. Multi-purpose rescue helmets
3. Rescue helmets

Candidate products:

- F2XT Rescue Helmet
- F3 Fire Helmet
- F1XF Fire Helmet
- F2XR Multi-Purpose Rescue Helmet

Notes:

- Fire helmets and rescue helmets should be separated from industrial hard hats.
- F1XF and F3 belong to firefighting helmet display.
- F2XT and F2XR belong to rescue / multi-purpose rescue helmet display.
- Product entries should verify listed standards, accessories, colors, and order numbers before publication.

References:

- `01-f2xt-f3-helmets.png`
- `02-f1xf-f2xr-helmets.png`

### 2. MSA Industrial Head Protection

Candidate products:

- V-Gard Standard Hat
- V-Gard 500 Hat

Notes:

- These should appear under industrial head protection, not fire helmets.
- Product cards should mention material options, colors, ventilation configuration, and accessory compatibility.

Reference:

- `03-vgard-hard-hats.png`

### 3. MSA Breathing Air Compressors

Candidate products:

- MSA 250H
- MSA 250HG
- MSA 300H
- MSA 300HG
- MSA 200T
- MSA 250T
- MSA 300T
- MSA 300TG
- MSA 100TE
- MSA 100TW
- MSA 100TB

Notes:

- These are compressor systems for high-pressure breathing air service.
- Keep them separate from SCBA wearer equipment.
- Product entries should verify flow rate, pressure, power, dimensions, and net weight before publication.

Reference:

- `04-high-pressure-breathing-air-compressors.png`

### 4. MSA Fire SCBA

Candidate products:

- M8-1800 self-contained positive pressure air breathing apparatus
- M8 self-contained positive pressure air breathing apparatus
- G1 full facepiece configurations

Notes:

- M8-1800 and M8 should be shown as core MSA fire SCBA product lines.
- G1 facepiece variants should be grouped as compatible facepiece configurations and accessories.
- Product entries should verify PN, cylinder configuration, pressure, facepiece size, HUD / IntelliHUD configuration, and standards before publication.

References:

- `05-m8-1800-scba.png`
- `06-m8-scba.png`
