## Display Order

### 1. Honeywell Respiratory Protection

Recommended sorting logic:

1. SCBA / self-contained compressed-air breathing apparatus
2. Facepiece and reducer configurations
3. Accessories

Candidate products:

- T8500 self-contained fire and industrial air breathing apparatus
- T8000 self-contained compressed-air breathing apparatus
- C900 self-contained compressed-air breathing apparatus

Notes:

- Keep these products separate from disposable respirators and cartridge respirators.
- T8500 should be positioned as a newer flagship Honeywell SCBA product.
- T8000 and C900 can be displayed as proven SCBA models or configuration families.
- Product entries should verify order numbers, cylinder configuration, standards, and facepiece details before publication.
