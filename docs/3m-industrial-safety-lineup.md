# 3M Industrial Safety Product Lineup

Date: 2026-06-16

This document records the 3M industrial safety products selected from the user-provided catalog screenshots. These products should be placed under the website's Industrial Safety product area, with 3M respiratory protection treated as a key product line.

Reference screenshots are archived in:

`docs/product-references/3m-industrial-safety/`

## Website Placement

Recommended navigation:

- Industrial Safety
- 3M Industrial Safety
- Respiratory Protection
- Hearing Protection
- Eye and Face Protection
- Fall Protection

3M Respiratory Protection should be shown before other 3M industrial safety products because it is a core product line.

## Accuracy Rule

These screenshots are accepted as source references, but final website product entries still need model-name, SKU, package quantity, standards, and image checks before publication. No product should be promoted as a featured official website product until the product image and SKU are confirmed against the catalog page.
